| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|

## Cost Optimization Tips

### Bundle Strategy
1. **Amazon.it Bundle**: Order items 1-6, 8-11 together = Free Prime shipping
2. **Thomann Bundle**: RODE mic + audio cables in one order = Free shipping
3. **Melopero Bundle**: Pi + display + microSD = Minimize shipping

### Price Tracking
- **CamelCamelCamel**: Track Amazon prices for mini-PCs
- **Keepa**: Browser extension for price history
- **Thomann Newsletter**: 10% first order discount sometimes

### Timing
- **Black Friday (Nov)**: Best prices on electronics
- **Prime Day (July)**: Good Amazon deals
- **Post-Christmas (Jan)**: Clearance on previous year models

## Risk Mitigation

### Stock Issues
| Item | Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------|------------|
| Raspberry Pi Zero 2W | HIGH | Buy immediately when in stock; check daily |
| Mini PC specific model | MEDIUM | Accept similar spec alternative |
| Hologram Pyramid | LOW | Many generic options available |

### Quality Issues
| Item | Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------|------------|
| Hologram Pyramid | HIGH | Buy from Amazon for easy returns |
| I2S Modules | MEDIUM | Order 2, keep spare |
| Generic Cables | LOW | Stick with known brands (Anker, UGreen) |

### Delivery Issues
| Vendor | Risk | Mitigation |
|--------|------|------------|
| AliExpress | HIGH | Only for non-critical items |
| Amazon.it | LOW | Prime = guaranteed dates |
| Melopero | LOW | Reliable Italian company |

## Order Checklist

### Before Ordering
- [ ] Compare prices across Amazon.it, .de, and Thomann
- [ ] Check stock availability
- [ ] Read recent reviews (last 3 months)
- [ ] Verify voltage compatibility (EU 220V)
- [ ] Confirm return policy

### After Ordering
- [ ] Save order confirmations
- [ ] Track shipments
- [ ] Test items within return window
- [ ] Keep packaging until confirmed working
